Little Lunch with Big Leaders


This series is my brainchild. It’s designed to give parents a chance to connect with principals before an open day, since our research showed principals heavily influence enrolment decisions. It's a 'get to know you' piece, not a sales pitch. It’s fun, aspirational, and made to show off the school and principal without boasting. Every episode opens with an audio recording of each school’s bell and kids playing, a small iykyk moment.

I run the whole process: sourcing and vetting talent out of 300 principals (checking for any bad press or issues I should know about), briefing them, and organising the shoot. On the day, I focus on making them comfortable. We walk around the school, I film them interacting with students, then we shoot the on-camera questions. The response has been fantastic, with great principal feedback and strong engagement online.

The Y Australia - Breakfast Program


This was an interstate production for the Global Y Council, covering one of Y NSW's initiatives. I reached out to the program's head of operations and a participating school, developed the concept, storyboard, shot list, and run sheet, and briefed all talent involved.

Given the sensitive nature of the program, it could indicate which students had come to school without food, so we worked closely with the school to protect student privacy. We flew to Brisbane for a two-day shoot: day one at the school, where we asked every student we saw whether they were comfortable being filmed (a big task given the school had over 1000 students), and I directed those who agreed; day two at the warehouse, where we balanced WHS standards with trying to capture genuine emotional moments from staff.
