Balor Australia

Balor Australia is a local, handmade beauty company making high-quality natural skincare. The brief was to capture what makes the brand special: products made with care, from ingredients that come from the natural world.

Nature, purity, and luxury

The challenge was designing something that felt premium without feeling cold. Natural origins front and centre, but with enough refinement to sit comfortably in the premium skincare space. Botanical elements and a considered colour palette do a lot of the heavy lifting here.
