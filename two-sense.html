Two Sense

Two Sense is a contemporary vintage fashion label founded by two sisters. The name is a nod to their father's saying about always contributing their "two cents." The brand needed to feel like them: considered, a little nostalgic, and genuinely original.

Where vintage meets contemporary

The coin illustration at the heart of the logo is a direct reference to the "two cents" etymology. Detailed, crafted, and full of character. The palette draws from a Wes Anderson-inspired world of warm, faded tones that feel timeless rather than trendy.
