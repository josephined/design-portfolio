Hands on Heart

Hands on Heart Australia is a non-profit organisation dedicated to teaching CPR — founded by an individual whose life was saved by CPR. Collaboratively, we transformed his vision into a brand that communicates care, urgency, and community connection.

A brand built on life

The logo needed to feel immediately recognisable — warm and human, yet carrying the urgency of a life-saving mission. The design centres hands in a gesture of care, directly referencing the CPR act while conveying community and connection. Every element was crafted to inspire trust and encourage action.
