Process

How I work

All motion work is produced in Adobe After Effects, with assets brought in from Illustrator and Photoshop. I start with the brand's visual language and find ways to translate static elements into movement — using timing and easing to give each animation its own personality.

Social-First

Designed for the feed

For social-first animations, I prioritise the first two seconds — designing for autoplay on muted feeds while ensuring the animation holds up with sound on.
