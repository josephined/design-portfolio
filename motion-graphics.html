Process

How I work

All motion work is produced in Adobe After Effects, with assets brought in from Illustrator and Photoshop. I start with the brand's visual language and find ways to translate static elements into movement. Timing and easing give each animation its own character. For social-first work, I design for the first two seconds. If it doesn't land on a muted feed, it doesn't work.
