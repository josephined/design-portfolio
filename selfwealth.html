Selfwealth

Selfwealth is a share‑trading platform designed to make investing accessible and understandable for everyday Australians. Over two years, the brand was strengthened through comprehensive design work, including two brand refreshes, a national out‑of‑home awareness campaign, social content, TikTok creative, motion graphics, and UX design.

Complex ideas made simple

Collaborating closely with marketing and product teams, complex financial concepts were reimagined into visual design that feels approachable, confident, and easy to understand. The focus was on making Selfwealth feel like an authority without being intimidating. Turning finance into something that actually makes sense to real people.
