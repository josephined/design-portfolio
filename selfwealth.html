Selfwealth

Selfwealth is a share trading platform that aims to make investing accessible for everyone. As their designer, my mission was to make finance captivating across two full brand refreshes, a national OOH campaign, animation, social media, and UX.

Complex ideas made simple

Partnering closely with marketing and product teams, I translated complex financial information into approachable, user-focused design -- helping reinforce Selfwealth's credibility and support customer growth within a highly regulated industry.
