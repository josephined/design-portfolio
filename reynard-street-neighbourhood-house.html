Reynard Street Neighbourhood House

While volunteering for the Reynard Street Neighbourhood House, I had the opportunity to design a fresh new take on their current branding — creating posters and marketing collateral that would genuinely appeal to local community members.

Design for the community

The challenge was to refresh an existing identity in a way that felt modern and welcoming without losing the warmth and familiarity the local community already associated with the house. The result was a approachable, vibrant visual direction for their print collateral and community-facing materials.
