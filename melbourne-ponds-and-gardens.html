Melbourne Ponds & Gardens

Melbourne Ponds and Gardens offers high-end pond cleaning and gardening services. The brief was to create a logo and brand identity that positions them as a serious, professional service without losing the high-end feeling.

Premium service, natural soul

The logo draws from organic forms and earthy tones. Natural, yet designed for a high-end customer. The identity works across vehicles, signage, and digital without losing anything in translation.
